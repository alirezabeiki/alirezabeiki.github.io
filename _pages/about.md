I am a computational fluid dynamics practitioner and researcher who is deeply fascinated by unsteady aerodynamics. I focus primarily on the physics of complex and time dependent flows in the context of rotorcraft, wind turbines, and emergent eVTOL platforms. My work involves understanding how we can model and control these flows more effectively.

During my PhD I broadened my knowledge on data driven methods and deep learning. I am now attempting to apply contemporary machine learning techniques to my areas of expertise to solve challenging aerodynamic problems. In addition to unsteady aerodynamics, my research interests involve aeroacoustics and flow control.

For my daily computational work I use sophisticated software packages, including ANSYS Fluent, ANSYS CFX, OpenFOAM, and STAR CCM+. I frequently develop code in Python, C++, Modern Fortran, and Julia for the purposes of scientific computing and software development.

You have the opportunity to investigate my publications, ongoing research projects, and open source codes throughout this website. I am consistently eager to engage in discussions with other researchers and explore novel concepts in the field of fluid mechanics.
